Apple's biggest product announcements of the year are arriving in September 2026, according to reports from [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com) and other tech publications. Here are the five products the company is about to release.
 
---
 
## iPhone Ultra
 
Apple's first foldable iPhone is launching this month. The device combines iPhone and iPad mini functionality in a single form factor with a [foldable display](https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/iphone-ultra/).
 
The iPhone Ultra features the new iOS 27 operating system optimized for the foldable screen. Specifications include the latest [Apple silicon](https://www.apple.com) processing power and support for all standard iPhone features on the expanded display.
 
Apple has delayed the foldable iPhone launch multiple times while perfecting the hardware and software integration. The iPhone Ultra represents the company's entry into the foldable smartphone market after years of development, with [multiple leaks](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/24/iphone-ultra-is-coming-six-new-features-in-apples-top-tier-model/) confirming hardware details.
 
---
 
## HomePad
 
The HomePad is [Apple's](https://www.apple.com/homepod/) new smart home hub, launching this year after delays related to [Siri AI](https://www.apple.com/siri/) development.
 
The device features Apple Photos slideshows, security camera functionality integrated into the home system, Siri AI voice control, and Apple Music playback with onscreen lyrics. The HomePad serves as the central hub for [Apple's](https://www.apple.com/home/) smart home ecosystem.
 
This is Apple's most significant smart home product since the original HomePod, representing a major shift in the company's [home device](https://www.theverge.com/21347551/apple-homekit-smart-home-guide-how-to) strategy.
 
---
 
## Apple TV 4K (New Model)
 
Apple is releasing an updated [Apple TV](https://www.apple.com/tv/), the first refresh in four years. The new model includes a more powerful chip, AI-driven features, and [software enhancements](https://www.apple.com/tvos/) powered by Siri AI.
 
The new Apple TV 4K supports enhanced streaming performance and improved content discovery through artificial intelligence. Updates to tvOS 26 bring new capabilities to the television experience.
 
There are reports the new model may include a camera for [FaceTime](https://www.apple.com/facetime/) and video calling on the big screen without requiring an iPhone.
 
---
 
## MacBook Ultra
 
The [MacBook Ultra](https://www.apple.com/macbook-pro/) is Apple's new laptop featuring significant hardware and software updates. The device includes [OLED display](https://www.apple.com) technology, touch screen support for [macOS](https://www.apple.com/macos/), a Dynamic Island, and a redesigned chassis.
 
New features include possible cellular connectivity options, bringing mobile connectivity to the MacBook line. The MacBook Ultra represents a major evolution in [Mac design](https://www.apple.com/mac/), incorporating features previously exclusive to [iPad Pro](https://www.apple.com/ipad-pro/).
 
The MacBook Ultra marks the first time a MacBook includes touch input alongside traditional trackpad and keyboard controls.
 
---
 
## Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4
 
Apple is introducing new [Apple Watch](https://www.apple.com/watch/) models with a new S-class chip built for artificial intelligence applications.
 
The [Series 12](https://www.apple.com/watch/) offers the standard model with updated processing power. The [Ultra 4](https://www.apple.com/watch/ultra/) represents the high-end sports variant with enhanced features for athletes and outdoor users.
 
Both models feature the new S-class processor, the first Apple Watch chip designed from the ground up for the AI era. The new chip enables [Siri AI](https://www.apple.com/siri/) features on the wrist and improved performance compared to previous generations.
 
Rumors indicate the new S-class chip will be the first meaningful performance improvement in [Apple Watch](https://www.apple.com/watch/) processors in three years.
 
---
 
## Availability
 
All five products are expected to launch in September 2026. [Apple](https://www.apple.com) typically announces new products during its [annual event](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/), with availability beginning shortly after the announcement.
 
Detailed specifications, pricing, and exact availability dates will be announced by [Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/) during the official launch event.
