As of September 1, 2026, the platform expanded to <a href="https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-ads-expands-across-europe/">52 countries including India, Europe, and the Middle East</a>. If you're an advertiser wondering whether to add ChatGPT to your budget, here's what you need to know about the platform, how it works in your market, and whether it's worth the spend.
 
## What ChatGPT Ads Actually Are
 
<a href="https://launchcodex.com/blog/performance-marketing/chatgpt-ads-breakdown/">ChatGPT Ads launched on February 9, 2026</a> as sponsored mentions inside ChatGPT conversations. When a user on a Free or Go plan asks ChatGPT for a recommendation, your ad appears as a clearly labeled sponsored card below the AI's answer — not a sidebar banner, but part of the conversation flow itself.
 
**The key insight:** Users don't switch tabs. They don't leave ChatGPT to see your ad. Your product recommendation appears right where they're already making a decision.
 
**Who sees ads:** Only Free and Go tier users (roughly 60-70% of ChatGPT's user base). Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise subscribers never see ads. This targeting is automatic — you don't select it.
 
**Ad format:** The only current format is the chat_card: a title, short description, image, and link below the AI response.

## ChatGPT Ads Manager: How It Works
 
<a href="https://ads.openai.com">ChatGPT Ads Manager opened as self-serve on July 22, 2026</a>, and expanded globally (including India) on August 31, 2026. The workflow is straightforward if you've run Google Ads or Meta Ads:
 
1. **Create a campaign** — Set budget, daily spend limit, and conversion goal
2. **Build ad creative** — Upload bulk via CSV or build directly in the platform
3. **Launch and monitor** — Track impressions, conversions, and clicks through weekly reporting
No API integration required. No minimum spend. Anyone with an OpenAI account can sign up.
 
**For larger advertisers:** <a href="https://artificialintelligence-news.com/news/chatgpt-ads-passes-1b-run-rate-in-200-days/">OpenAI's Ads Solutions team also handles enterprise deals through managed services and agency partners</a>.

## ChatGPT Ads in India: What Changed
 
India is one of the <a href="https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/where-chatgpt-ads-are-sold-every-market-and-date/">52 countries where self-serve Ads Manager launched on August 31, 2026</a>. This matters because:
 
- Previously, Indian advertisers could only reach ChatGPT users through OpenAI's sales team (high minimum, long sales cycle)
- Now, any registered Indian business can sign up at ads.openai.com and launch campaigns immediately
- Pricing, audience size, and performance data all apply to India the same as the US and Europe
**India-specific metrics:** <a href="https://flisko.si/en/blog/chatgpt-ads-guide-2026/">ChatGPT's Free and Go tier users in India represent a high-intent audience researching products, planning purchases, and comparing options</a>.

## ChatGPT Ads Pricing: What to Budget
 
<a href="https://launchcodex.com/blog/performance-marketing/chatgpt-ads-breakdown/">Pricing has evolved dramatically:</a>
 
- **February 2026:** $60 CPM (cost per 1,000 impressions), $200,000 minimum
- **May 2026:** $25 CPM (average), minimum removed
- **September 2026:** CPM varies by category ($2-$5 CPC floors for click-based bidding)
**Translation:** You can test ChatGPT Ads with as little as ₹500-1,000 daily budget in India. No minimum commitment.
 
For ecommerce, <a href="https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/chatgpt-ads-september-2026-product-feeds-now-required-platform-expands-globally-and-the-new-tools-ecommerce-brands-need-right-now/">ChatGPT now requires product feeds for shopping results</a>, similar to Google Shopping.

## Who Should Advertise on ChatGPT (And Who Shouldn't)
 
**Best fit for:**
- B2B software (high intent, decision-stage buyers)
- Premium consumer brands (Best Buy, Lowe's, VistaPrint already run campaigns)
- Ecommerce with strong product feeds
- Services (legal, financial, recruiting — people research these inside ChatGPT)
**Skip if:**
- Your product is still unknown (awareness advertising doesn't work here)
- You can't measure ROI (weekly CSV reporting only, no real-time dashboards yet)
- Budget under ₹50,000/month (better to test with Google/Meta first)
