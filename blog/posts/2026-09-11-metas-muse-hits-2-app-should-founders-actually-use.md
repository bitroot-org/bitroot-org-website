<a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/10/metas-ai-agent-muse-is-now-the-no-2-app-in-the-us/">Meta's Muse hit the No. 2 spot on the iOS App Store</a> within 48 hours of launch. <a href="https://forkast.news/metas-muse-launches-as-the-biggest-cross-app-ai-agent-yet-with-a-security-disclosure-problem">Muse, which launched September 8, 2026</a>, racked up over 83,000 iOS downloads in the United States. But the headline obscures a more important question for founders: Does the ranking mean Muse is actually useful, and should you be testing it?
 
## What Muse Actually Does (Unlike Claude or ChatGPT)
 
Muse is not a chatbot. <a href="https://tech-insider.org/what-meta-actually-shipped-with-muse/">It's a personal AI agent designed to execute tasks across your digital life: email, calendar, payments, shopping, health data, smart home devices, and more</a>.
 
The distinction matters. ChatGPT and Claude answer questions and help you think through problems. Muse actually takes actions on your behalf — scheduling meetings, placing orders, filling out forms, managing your calendar — without you doing the manual work.
 
**Pricing:** Free tier (limited), $20/month Power tier, $100/month Maximum tier.
 
**Availability:** US-only at launch, 18+ only.
 
**Security model:** <a href="https://forkast.news/metas-muse-launches-as-the-biggest-cross-app-ai-agent-yet-with-a-security-disclosure-problem">Runs in a dedicated Muse Secure VM with a Sentinel agent that monitors actions and prompts authorization for high-risk tasks</a>.
 
## The Reality Behind the #2 Ranking
 
Yes, Muse hit #2. But the context matters. <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/10/metas-ai-agent-muse-is-now-the-no-2-app-in-the-us/">ChatGPT hit 500,000 downloads in its first week (averaging 83,300 daily); Muse took two days to hit 83,000 downloads</a>. Threads launched with 4.3 million downloads on day one. Meta AI saw 108,000 at launch.
 
Muse's ranking is real, but it's a slower climb than Meta's other launches. Part of that is trust — people are hesitant to give an AI agent access to their email, payments, and calendar. Part of it is timing — the app arrived days after Meta's privacy issues surfaced, which naturally creates friction.
 
## Should Founders Test Muse?
 
That depends on what you're trying to do.
 
**Test Muse if:**
- You spend hours on repetitive tasks (email triage, scheduling, form-filling, expense reports)
- You need something that actually executes actions, not just advises
- You're comfortable giving Meta broad access to your digital life
- You want to understand where the agentic AI space is headed
**Skip if:**
- You're just looking for a better ChatGPT (Claude Pro or ChatGPT Plus are better for that)
- You're uncomfortable with Meta's data practices
- Your workflows don't involve cross-app integrations (Muse's main value)
- You're on Android (it's currently only #338 in Productivity on Google Play)
## The Bigger Picture
 
Muse's #2 ranking signals real founder interest in AI agents that execute, not just explain. The slower adoption than previous Meta launches signals hesitation around privacy and trust.
 
If you're evaluating personal AI agents — whether Muse, Claude, or OpenAI's offerings — the decision hinges on a simple framework: Do you need reasoning and analysis (Claude/ChatGPT), or do you need task execution (Muse)?
