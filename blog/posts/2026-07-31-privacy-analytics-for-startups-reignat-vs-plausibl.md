You're a founder with a SaaS that's making real money. Maybe $50K MRR. Maybe $200K. You need analytics—visitors, funnels, conversions. But Google Analytics makes you uncomfortable. It tracks users across the web, feeds an ad machine, and now GDPR fines can hit six figures.
 
So you look for alternatives. You find three names: Reignat, Plausible, Fathom. All privacy-first. All GDPR-compliant. None explain which one is actually right for your stage.
 
Here's what matters.
 
## The Problem With Google Analytics (For Bootstrapped Teams)
 
<cite index="33-1">Google Analytics is a data-hoovering machine. It tracks users across sites, builds profiles, and feeds the ad machine. That's fine for big corps. But for indie founders, it feels off. Plus, regulations like GDPR and CCPA are tightening. Fines can hit six figures.</cite>
 
Your bootstrap budget can't absorb a six-figure fine. You also don't need enterprise-grade dashboards. You need: real-time visitors, top pages, conversion funnels, user journeys. Nothing more.
 
Privacy-first analytics deliver exactly that—without the legal risk.
 
## The Comparison
 
### **Reignat: Best for Real-Time Simplicity**
 
<cite index="32-1">Reignat is a privacy-friendly web analytics platform built for founders, startups, and indie makers who want clear insights without the complexity of traditional analytics tools. Track visitors, pageviews, traffic sources, devices, locations, funnels, and user journeys in real time.</cite> ([reignat.com](https://reignat.com))
 
**What you get:**
- Real-time visitor globe
- Conversion funnels + user journeys
- AI trend insights
- Embeddable widget
- Team roles
**Pricing:** Starts at $29/month  
**Setup time:** 15 minutes (script injection)  
**Best for:** Founders who want to *feel* traction immediately
 
**Trade-off:** Newer product, smaller team
 
---
 
### **Plausible: Best for Lightweight Simplicity**
 
Open-source foundation with managed option. No cookie banner. Dashboard loads in <1 second. ([plausible.io](https://plausible.io))
 
**What you get:**
- Pageviews, bounce rate, top pages
- Traffic sources + device breakdown
- Simplest UI of the three
**Pricing:** $10-20/month (or self-host free)  
**Setup time:** 10 minutes  
**Best for:** Founders who hate complexity (truly minimal)
 
**Trade-off:** Limited to basic metrics—no user journeys or funnels on base plan. Funnels require the $20+ tier.
 
---
 
### **Fathom: Best for Mid-Stage Growth**
 
Designed for teams scaling from $0-$10M ARR. Faster query speeds than competitors. Includes goals/events tracking by default. ([usefathom.com](https://usefathom.com))
 
**What you get:**
- Real-time dashboard (like Reignat)
- Custom events + goals
- Heatmaps + session recordings (premium)
- Integrations (Zapier, Slack, webhooks)
- White-label option
**Pricing:** Starts at $14/month (basic), $29+ for goals  
**Setup time:** 20 minutes  
**Best for:** Founders who will outgrow basic analytics in 6-12 months
 
**Trade-off:** Slightly more complex than Plausible. Session recordings cost extra.

## The Recommendation (By Stage)
 
**Just launched (<$10K MRR):** Use **Plausible** ($10/mo). You need visitors + sources. Nothing else matters yet.
 
**Early traction ($10K-50K MRR):** Use **Reignat** ($29/mo). Real-time funnels matter now. You want to *see* progress. AI insights help identify patterns before they're obvious.
 
**Scaling ($50K+ MRR):** Use **Fathom** ($29/mo). You need session recordings, integrations, and custom events. You can afford the extra complexity because your team is now two people instead of one.

## Setup Checklist (5 Minutes)
 
1. Sign up to your chosen platform
2. Add script to website header (or use tag manager)
3. Wait 24 hours for first data
4. Set up one conversion goal (form submit, checkout click)
5. Add teammates (if applicable)
That's it. GDPR-compliant. No consent banner needed (for these tools—check your privacy policy to be sure).

**Ready to move off Google Analytics?** Start with Plausible if you're bootstrapped. Switch to Reignat when real-time matters. Move to Fathom when you have revenue to optimize.
