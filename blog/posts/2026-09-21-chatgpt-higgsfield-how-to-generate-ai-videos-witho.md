## How to Connect Higgsfield to ChatGPT: 3-Step Setup
 
**Step 1: Open ChatGPT Plugins Directory**
 
Go to ChatGPT (web or desktop app) and open the Plugins Directory. You'll find it in the left sidebar under "Explore GPTs" or in the main chat interface as a tool selector.
 
**Step 2: Find and Add Higgsfield**
 
Search for "Higgsfield" in the directory, or visit [higgsfield.ai/mcp](https://higgsfield.ai/mcp) and click "Add Higgsfield plugin." You'll be prompted to authorize with your Higgsfield account. If you don't have one, create it (free signup is available).
 
**Step 3: Start Creating**
 
Start a new conversation, mention Higgsfield in your prompt, or select it from the available tools. Then describe what you want to make: "Generate a 10-second product demo for my AI tool. Make it look cinematic, with a clean product shot and a hand reveal." ChatGPT handles the rest.
 
**Total time: 3 minutes.** You're not paying extra for the plugin. It uses your existing Higgsfield credits.

## When Higgsfield in ChatGPT Wins vs When It Loses
 
**Higgsfield wins when:**
- You want fast iteration (brief → video in one thread)
- You're making short-form content (Reels, Shorts, TikTok, LinkedIn)
- Character consistency matters (Soul ID works well)
- You value model variety (50+ image, 10+ video models)
- Your workflow ends at generation (no heavy editing)
- You're bootstrapped and need speed over control
**Runway wins when:**
- Post-production editing is part of your workflow (Director Mode, Motion Brush)
- You need 4K output (Higgsfield maxes at 1080p)
- Reliability matters more than speed (production studios prefer Runway's stability)
- You want proprietary models (Gen-4.5, Gen-4 Turbo) not available elsewhere
- Your team is comfortable with a separate editing timeline
- You need audio generation built-in
**Honest gap:** If your workflow is brief → video → edit in DaVinci or Premiere, you'll probably use both. Higgsfield for generation, Runway or native editors for polish.

## What You Need to Know Before Starting
 
1. **Every generation uses credits.** Signing in to ChatGPT from Higgsfield uses the same account and the same credits as generating on higgsfield.ai directly. No separate pools.
2. **The plugin doesn't cover everything.** Audio generation, website building, and real-time editing are desktop-only. If you need those, use standalone Higgsfield.
3. **ChatGPT expands your prompts.** You say "generate a product demo," ChatGPT asks clarifying questions and writes a full production prompt automatically. This usually improves results but adds ~30 seconds per generation.
4. **Iteration happens in chat.** Want to try the same clip with a different color grading or camera angle? Describe the change, ChatGPT regenerates. Each regeneration costs new credits.
5. **Setup differences from Claude.** In Claude, Higgsfield connects as an MCP connector with a custom URL. In ChatGPT, it's an official plugin (no URL needed). Same credits, different setup.
